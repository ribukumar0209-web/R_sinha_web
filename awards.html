Special Achievements

Antarctica Scientific Expedition

Participated in the XVI Indian expedition to Antarctica, December'96 – April'97 and executed a project on hydrology and sedimentology of Priyadarshini Lake, Schirmacher Oasis, Antarctica. Awarded 1998.

Film Direction: SAVE SOIL

Anchored and directed a film on "SAVE SOIL, USE FLYASH" (2000, 17.20 mins). Produced by TV Centre, IIT Kanpur; sponsored by Flyash Mission, DST, New Delhi.
