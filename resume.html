Academic Trajectory

Education & Professional Background

A comprehensive chronological timeline of degrees, academic positions, and institutional leadership roles.

Educational Qualifications

  • Nov 2000 - Nov 2001 & May-July 2002: Post Doctoral Research, University of Karlsruhe, Germany. Late Quaternary history and paleoclimatic reconstruction in Thar desert through lacustrine sequences in western Rajasthan, India.
  • 1989 - 1992: Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.), University of Cambridge, UK. Fluvial Geomorphology and Sedimentology.
  • 1984 - 1987: Master of Technology (M.Tech), University of Roorkee, India. Applied Geology.
  • 1981 - 1984: Bachelor of Science (B.Sc. Hons.), Patna University, India. Geology Honours.
  • 1979 - 1981: Intermediate in Science (I.Sc.), Patna University, India.
  • 1979: Secondary School Certificate, Bihar Secondary Exam. Board, India.

Professional Experience

  • Aug 2014 - Present: HAG Professor, Department of Earth Sciences, IIT Kanpur.
  • April 2014 - July 2014: Professor, Department of Earth Sciences, IIT Kanpur.
  • Dec 2005 - April 2014: Professor, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • Oct 2001 - Dec 2005: Associate Professor, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • Dec 1995 - Sept 2001: Assistant Professor, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • June 1994 - Nov 1995: Lecturer, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • April 1993 - June 1994: Scientist, National Institute of Hydrology, Roorkee.

Administrative Experience

  • Oct 2011 - Sept 2012: Chairman, Senate Post-Graduate Committee (SPGC), IIT Kanpur.
  • Sept 2011 - Aug 2012: DPGC Convener, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • Aug 2006 - Nov 2007: Chairman, Students' Placement Committee, IIT Kanpur.
  • Oct 2006 - Sept 2007: DPGC Convener, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • May 2004 - 2010: Professor In-Charge, Geosciences Laboratory, Civil Engg., IIT Kanpur.
  • Oct 2003 - Sept 2005: Member, Senate Library Committee, IIT Kanpur.
  • Oct 2002 - Sept 2003: Chairman, Senate Library Committee, IIT Kanpur.
  • June 1999 - Nov 2000: Computer Coordinator (CE) and Member CCCC, IIT Kanpur.
  • Aug 1996 - July 1997: Member, DPGC, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • Aug 1994 - July 1996: Member, DUGC, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kanpur.
  • March 1994 - March 1995: Warden, Hall V, IIT Kanpur.
