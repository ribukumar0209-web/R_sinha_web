Outreach Activities

Selected academic, scientific and professional outreach activities spanning training, workshops, conferences and specialised programmes.

TerraAqua UAV Pvt. Ltd.

Founder

TerrAqua UAV, founded in 2018, is a Geospatial Intelligence company specializing in high-resolution drone and satellite data.

We design and develop advanced geospatial products and apply advanced Earth observation technologies and Remote Sensing analytics to deliver Sustainable actionable insights across sectors such as: Natural resource management, Agriculture, Environment conservation, Disaster risk reduction, and Urban Resilience.
