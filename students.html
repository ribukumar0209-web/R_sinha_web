Ankit Yadav

Sediment dynamics

I am a Research Scholar in Earth Sciences at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur), working on sediment...I am a Research Scholar in Earth Sciences at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur), working on sediment dynamics, geomorphic processes, and cascading natural hazards in the Himalayan mountain systems. My doctoral research focuses on developing an integrated geospatial and process-based framework to quantify sediment flux, erosion dynamics, and multi-hazard interactions, including landslides, debris flows, and floods in the Alaknanda Basin, Garhwal Himalaya. My work combines UAV-based remote sensing, LiDAR analysis, terrain morphometry, field investigations, and numerical hazard modeling using GIS, Python, R, SAR and Google Earth Engine to support hazard risk assessment and disaster risk reduction.
