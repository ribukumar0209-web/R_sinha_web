Research Domains & Specialties

Pioneering multi-disciplinary investigations across fluvial geomorphology, climate hydrology, and surface dynamics.

Quaternary Geology

Paleoclimatic reconstruction from fluvial and lacustrine records; long-term sedimentation history in Himalayan foreland basin using drill cores.
Learn More

River Science

River dynamics, geomorphic diversity, environmental flows and river health, floodplain lakes/wetlands and their restoration, critical zones in river basins.
Learn More

Geohazards

Flood risk assessment and management, landslides and flash floods.
Learn More

Polar Science

Antarctic lakes – hydrogeochemistry, sedimentary environments and paleoclimatic reconstruction.
Learn More

Remote Sensing & GIS

Satellite remote sensing and drone-based optical, thermal and hyperspectral imaging for water quality assessment and terrain characterisation.
Learn More

Fluvial Sedimentology & Stratigraphy

Late Quaternary stratigraphic development, channel avulsion mechanics, core archive sedimentology, and basin architecture in Himalayan and peninsular river systems.
